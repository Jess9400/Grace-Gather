# Community prayer requests

Adds Request a prayer beneath Talk to God in both Home invitation states. The profile menu exposes Prayer requests and Notifications; the existing celestial avatar shows the unread prayer-alert count. Notifications are in-app only and opt-in, per the user's choice. No email or phone push is sent.

Members provide a theme and reason, choose anonymous or their display name, optionally schedule a shared time, preview, then explicitly share for moderator review. Dates are entered in the device's local time zone and stored as UTC; other members see their own local time. Future requests must be 5 minutes–30 days ahead. Immediate requests say Pray whenever you can. Existing staff review requests in the prayer screen. Reasons for rejection are private to the author and moderators.

Only approved requests are visible to others. A member can confirm once per request, cannot confirm their own prayer, and cannot confirm a scheduled prayer early. Counts describe confirmed responses, not church presence, notification delivery, or RSVP intent. Authors can close requests. Reports hide requests for the reporter and expose them to staff review. Accounts are linked internally even for anonymous posts, but author UUIDs and response identities are never returned in member-facing cards.

Opted-in members receive an invitation on approval and one in-app reminder at the scheduled time. A one-minute database cron generates reminders; foreground UI refresh is every 30 seconds. In-app notices do not wake a phone. Opt-out stops new notices and marks existing notices read; history remains available. Authors who opt in receive a consolidated notice when another member confirms prayer. Closed/rejected/expired requests disappear from other members' alerts. Requests remain active for seven days after their prayer time. Lists are bounded to the latest 50 items for this first version.

Backend: five private tables, default-deny RLS, owner/staff-checked RPCs, cascade cleanup on account deletion, three new requests per account per day, unique member/request confirmation constraint. No public writes or direct access to private tables. The Supabase advisor flags the intentional authenticated SECURITY DEFINER entrypoints; every entrypoint enforces identity and participation and every sensitive action checks ownership/staff status. Guidance: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/database-linter?lint=0029_authenticated_security_definer_function_executable

Validation: typecheck, lint, web export, 28 mobile tests and 32 backend tests including existing community/confession protections. Prayer-specific tests exercise anonymous privacy, pending visibility, moderator-only approval, opt-in/out, duplicate/self/early confirmations, report hiding, closure, UTC conversion and once-only reminders. Migration deployed; production has zero prayer requests, all five tables have RLS, cron is active, and anon cannot execute submission RPCs. No fake member activity or notifications were created. Signed-in phone/browser end-to-end notification delivery remains a release check; automated tests cover the database flow.

App changes stay in a review PR rather than merging main automatically. Independent of Bible Study PR #40.
