## Shared preparation: useful regardless of program

- [ ] Agree on a short, truthful problem statement and who the app serves; distinguish individual spiritual support from clinical or emergency care.
- [ ] Gather consent-based, non-identifying evidence of demand outside the founders' immediate network: interviews, pilot feedback and repeat usage if available. Do not fabricate metrics or testimonials.
- [ ] Prepare a short demo using synthetic accounts and content. Show what already works, what is in testing and what is still planned.
- [ ] Finish the current iPhone and Android tests; prioritize sign-in, onboarding, journal privacy and sync, consent, moderated interactions, reporting, notifications and accessibility. Track failures rather than describing a test as passed prematurely.
- [ ] Prepare an accurate AI and privacy explanation: what is sent to an external provider with consent, retention and deletion, human moderation, limitations, and safe support for vulnerable people. Confirm rights for Bible editions, art, audio and source content.
- [ ] Draft a simple budget and sustainability options, including AI and infrastructure costs, without choosing subscription pricing or changing the user's free-plan decision yet. Check program terms for fees, grants, equity, tax and recurring costs.
- [ ] Agree on team roles, ownership and licensing with Taya and any other collaborators; confirm any public demo, application materials or program sharing is authorized.
- [ ] Record actual founder/venture location, target market and legal structure, and check each program's geographic and entity eligibility before applying. Plan travel only after costs and dates are confirmed.
