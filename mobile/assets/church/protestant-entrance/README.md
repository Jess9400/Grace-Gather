# Protestant church entrance

Shown instead of the 3D wooden doors when the person's tradition is
Protestant: a simple modern church, because grand stone doors feel foreign,
even pretentious, to many Protestants. Drawn for Grace & Gather; original
artwork, not a copy of any real church.

- `background.png`: dusk sky, a plain rendered hall with a low pitched roof,
  a slim tower with a plain lit cross, wide warm windows, a flat canopy, two
  small door lights, and the warm lobby seen through the glass doors.
- `door-left.png`, `door-right.png`: the two glass doors with dark frames and
  brass pull handles, drawn to sit exactly over the doorway so they can swing
  open.

All three are drawn in a 390 × 844 frame and exported at 3×. The SVG sources
are in `svg/`; `svg/draw.py` writes them. The doorway is the rectangle
x 150–240, y 596–736 of that frame.
