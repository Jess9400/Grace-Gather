# Moderator safety review

Grace & Gather is not an emergency service. Journals are private and are not monitored for emergencies. No one should promise a response time, a rescue, or 24-hour coverage that the team cannot provide.

## Before you begin

- The team must agree who is watching the moderation dashboard and when. If nobody is available, there is no coverage.
- Sign in with a moderator account, enable moderator phone alerts on your own device, and send a synthetic test submission. Push delivery can fail or be delayed; also check the dashboard yourself.
- Keep access restricted to authorized moderators. Do not copy personal stories, recordings, screenshots, or names into group chats or incident notes.

## When a possible safety concern appears

1. Open the moderation dashboard. Review highlighted sections first, including Confession, discussions, prayers, testimonies, stories, Pay it forward, and reported chats. Read or listen to the full submission. A flag may be incorrect, and not every report is an emergency. Review every queue too: the priority section cannot catch every concern.
2. Do not treat grief, illness, a mention of suicide, religious writing, or a request for help as misconduct by itself. Seek another moderator's judgment when needed. You cannot approve your own confession.
   Chat reports show only the reported message and the reporter's reason, not the surrounding conversation. Do not assume missing context.
3. If an appropriate communication channel is available, encourage the person to reach local emergency services or a trusted person if they may be in immediate danger. The confession moderation screen does not itself provide a direct message channel.
4. If you have a credible reason to believe someone faces imminent danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service for the person's location when it is known. Give only relevant information you already have, and follow a documented and legally reviewed disclosure procedure. Do not promise responders can identify or locate an anonymous member. Do not contact police automatically based solely on an AI flag.
5. Record a brief decision and the action taken without copying the person's content or other unnecessary personal details. Observe any legally required reporting or preservation rules. Ask another moderator if uncertain.

## Current limits

- Moderator phone notifications for reports and safety flags are distinguishable but never include private content. Delivery can fail or be delayed. Review lists may be bounded; push is not an emergency dispatch system.
- AI flags are captured only when the relevant AI permission and screening are active. Audio flags require the separate recording permission; transcripts are not stored in the safety flag. Private chats are not automatically screened: only reported messages enter a moderator queue. Live gathering audio is not monitored or recorded for review; members may send a written report. Review all queues and reports, including anything received without AI permission.
- AI and keyword checks can miss context and make mistakes. An absence of flags is not evidence that a person is safe.
- Before public launch, review child-safety reporting, emergency disclosure, privacy wording, and records preservation for Brazil and the U.S. with an appropriate local adviser. Test moderator permissions, phone alerts, and escalation using synthetic content on real devices.
