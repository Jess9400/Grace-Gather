# Matos Soares study-reader source review

The study reader is being prepared using the 73-book JSON at https://github.com/bibliacatolica/biblia, source commit `f0c849ce9340ccd4686859451203c679f8cb9052`, file SHA-256 `a66118c41f02b1f589b25c792dd7fab247e93b0f8b94a17985b2c1ecb788ce82`. The source says its digital curation is CC0, and claims the underlying translation is public domain. That claim has not been independently verified and the dataset does not identify a specific scanned edition.

Two passages (Matthew 11:28 and Psalm 22) match text on pages identifying it as the 1956 edition, even though the source describes a 1927/1932 Vulgate translation. Those samples alone do not prove the edition of the entire dataset. An academic study reports the translator died in 1957, while the source says 1955. Brazil's general copyright rule is 70 years from January 1 after the author's death; the applicable rights, contributors, edition, and distribution territories need independent review. CC0 for a digital compilation does not settle underlying text rights.
