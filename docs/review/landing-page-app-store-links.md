# Landing page: App Store links

Complete this checklist after the Grace & Gather landing page is published. The
addresses below are proposed destinations, not confirmation that the pages are
live. Keep this PR open until the links have been checked and entered in App
Store Connect.

## Support URL

- [ ] Publish a page on `graceandgather.online` that explains how to contact
  support. The landing page itself can serve this purpose if the contact
  information is easy to find.
- [ ] Display `hello@graceandgather.online` and verify that a message sent from
  another account arrives in the support inbox. Forwarding was tested on
  14 November 2026; check again when the site is live.
- [ ] Open the page without signing in and check that the contact information is
  readable on a phone.
- [ ] Enter the verified page address in **App Store Connect → Grace & Gather →
  Distribution → iOS App Version → Support URL**, and save.

## Privacy Policy URL

- [ ] Finish reviewing the policy for the app's actual data collection,
  third-party services, retention, deletion, backups, and all supported platforms.
  The current policy remains an early-access draft; publishing it does not by
  itself complete that review.
- [ ] Publish the reviewed policy on the landing-page domain, for example at
  `https://graceandgather.online/privacy`. The exact path can change, but the
  App Store link should lead directly to the complete policy.
- [ ] Verify the link works publicly without a login and that the policy is
  accessible from within the app as well as from the landing page.
- [ ] Enter the verified policy address in **App Store Connect → Grace & Gather
  → App Privacy → Privacy Policy URL**, and save.
- [ ] Complete the separate App Privacy questionnaire using the verified data
  practices of the app and its third-party services.

## Final check

- [ ] Reopen both saved links from App Store Connect and confirm they lead to the
  right public pages. Check the in-app privacy link and policy match the
  published version.
- [ ] Keep the app in preparation until the remaining release checks are done.
  These steps do not submit it for review or publish it.

References: [Apple's app privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)
and [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
