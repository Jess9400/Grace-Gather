# Orthodox church entrance

Shown instead of the 3D wooden doors when the person's tradition is Orthodox.
Drawn for Grace & Gather; original artwork, not a copy of any real church.

- `background.png`: dusk sky, white church with a golden onion dome and an
  eight-pointed cross, an icon above the door, warm lit windows, and the warm
  doorway with a glimpse of the icon screen inside.
- `door-left.png`, `door-right.png`: the two oak leaves, drawn to sit exactly
  over the doorway so they can swing open.

All three are drawn in a 390 × 844 frame and exported at 3×. The SVG sources
are in `svg/`; `svg/draw.py` writes them. The doorway is the rectangle
x 150–240, y 547–736 of that frame.
